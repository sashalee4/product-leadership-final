# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** the app field teams use every step of their day
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** contractors, US based, large projects
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** easy offline to online settings to avoid losing work and ability to communicate with risk mitigation based on documented work, timestamps and pictures from the field teams
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Buy/partner for offline network/extended network for remote build sites, build functionality that doesn't ask users to do more and helps them work faster and smarter
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** monthly retention review, quarterly product retrospectives, onsite discovery sessions with field teams

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will own photo-driven field documentation and daily reporting for superintendents. We will not invest in additional enterprise analytics until field adoption reaches a defined threshold

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Win daily usage in the field
- **KR1:** Increase Weekly Active Field Users from 22 to 53%
- **KR2:** Reduce average time spent on daily reporting and documentation by 50%
- **KR3:** Have 50% of active projects generate field activity at least 5 days per week

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** Highlighting that I might need to focus on incentives for use and not just usability
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** I will adjust one of my OKRs that was more focused on a result to one that is focused on incentivizing behavior

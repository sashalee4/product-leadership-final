# Meridian Now / Next / Later Roadmap

## Strategy

For field superintendents and foremen, Meridian becomes the fastest and most trusted way to document work, communicate progress, and protect themselves from disputes, without adding administrative burden.

## Quarterly Outcomes

- **KR1:** Increase Weekly Active Field Users from 22% to 53%
- **KR2:** Reduce average time spent on daily reporting and documentation by 50%
- **KR3:** Have 50% of active projects generate field activity at least 5 days per week

## Roadmap

| Now | Next | Later — bets, not commitments |
|---|---|---|
| **We bet making Meridian reliable at any job site will increase weekly usage and consistent field activity for foremen and superintendents.** *(KR1, KR3)* | **Prove sustained habit formation** across active projects.<br><br>*Why next:* Confirm Now is creating repeat behavior, not one-time adoption. | **Bet on proactive dispute prevention** by surfacing missing documentation and unresolved risks before they become claims. |
| **We bet making Meridian the fastest way to document work and communicate progress will displace phone cameras, texts, and WhatsApp for field teams.** *(KR1, KR3)* | **Make project records trustworthy by default.**<br><br>*Why next:* Speed and simplicity only matter if teams trust the resulting record. | **Bet on voice- and photo-assisted documentation** to reduce typing and structured input. |
| **We bet removing unnecessary reporting effort while preserving essential project evidence will cut documentation time by 50% for foremen.** *(KR2)* | **Connect field activity to project-office workflows.**<br><br>*Why next:* Make field updates valuable to PMs without adding work for foremen. | **Bet on intelligent project summaries** that turn field activity into reliable progress narratives. |
